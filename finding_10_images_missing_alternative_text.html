Experiment design

How to prove it worked

Two-proportion z-test at 95% significance and 80% power. Halving the minimum detectable effect roughly quadruples the sample required, which is why the conservative figure is so much larger.

Run until the calculated sample is reached AND at least one full business cycle has elapsed; 2-4 weeks is the practical norm.
