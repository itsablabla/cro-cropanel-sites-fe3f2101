ProblemThe consent popup appears un-dismissable, blocking the entire page content and forcing users to sign up or leave. This traps users, increases frustration, and likely raises bounce rate. The lack of a close button violates user autonomy and erodes trust in the brand. The popup is above the fold and immediately noticeable, making it a high-impact leak.
RECOMMENDED — Dismissible banner
✓ Dismissible bannerConsent banner is now dismissible with a clear close button, avoiding content blocking and preserving user autonomy.

Homepage

Shop All
We use cookies to improve your experience. AcceptDecline
FixReplace the blocking popup with a non-intrusive, dismissible consent banner. Add a clear close button and allow users to decline or accept cookies without forcing sign-up. Ensure the main content (headline and CTA) is immediately visible and accessible. This preserves user autonomy and reduces friction, likely improving conversion and trust.
