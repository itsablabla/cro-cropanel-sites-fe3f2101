Method

How this audit was produced

7 independent analysts reviewed 6 pages of allbirds.com crawled on August 2026. Six examine the page heuristically: funnel path, message clarity, form friction, trust and objections, pricing transparency, and navigational distraction. The seventh takes direct technical measurements. Findings were merged, de-duplicated, scored with PXL and sequenced into the roadmap.

Read every finding as a hypothesis Without analytics access, a heuristic finding identifies where value is likely leaking, not proof that it is. That is exactly why PXL scores the four evidence questions at zero for those findings. Anything marked Verified by measurement is a different class of claim: it was measured directly and is reproducible.
