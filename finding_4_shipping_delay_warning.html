ProblemThe prominent warning 'orders may take up to 30 days to ship' in the top bar creates immediate purchase anxiety. It highlights a potential delay before the user even sees the product, undermining trust in the brand's delivery promise. This negative framing can increase cart abandonment and reduce conversion, especially for time-sensitive shoppers. The warning is visible sitewide, amplifying its negative impact across the entire funnel.
RECOMMENDED — Positive shipping
✓ Free shippingFree shipping on orders over $100.

New Arrivals

Wildly Comfortable. Super Natural.

Experience the softest, most sustainable fabrics.

Shop All
FixReplace the negative shipping warning with a positive, benefit-oriented message: 'Free shipping on orders over $100.' This reframes the shipping experience as a value-add, reducing anxiety and increasing perceived value. If the delay notice is still necessary, move it to a less prominent location such as the checkout page, where it is less likely to deter initial engagement. This change directly addresses the trust objection and encourages users to proceed with their purchase.
