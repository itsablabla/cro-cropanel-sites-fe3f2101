ProblemThe homepage hero presents multiple competing CTAs (Shop All, Shop Womens, Shop Mens, etc.) without a single clear primary action. This dilutes user focus and creates decision paralysis, as users are forced to evaluate many options before taking any action. The lack of a dominant call-to-action reduces the likelihood of conversion because users may leave without committing to a specific path.
RECOMMENDED — Single Primary CTA
Homepage Hero

✓ Single Primary CTAOne clear action reduces decision fatigue and focuses the user.
Shop Best Sellers Shop Women's Shop Men's
FixReduce the hero to a single primary CTA, 'Shop Best Sellers', which is a high-intent action that guides users toward the most popular products. De-emphasize secondary CTAs (Shop Women's, Shop Men's) by styling them as secondary buttons and moving them below the fold or into a less prominent position. This creates a clear visual hierarchy, reduces cognitive load, and focuses the user on one action, increasing the probability of conversion.
